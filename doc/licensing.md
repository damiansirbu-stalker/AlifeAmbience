# AlifeAmbience source licensing

The license of every sound source AlifeAmbience ships from. The mod ships only free content or content
whose author granted permission. The readme Licensing section is derived from this record.

## Scope caveat - permissions were granted for AlifeSpooks

The permission grants recorded below were obtained for AlifeSpooks, a different mod. A permission scoped
to one mod does not automatically extend to another. Public distribution of AlifeAmbience's audio -
including a public GitHub repo - is redistribution and requires each permission-granted author to extend
their grant to AlifeAmbience specifically.

Until every bed source is cleared for AlifeAmbience, this repo is PRIVATE and its audio is not publicly
redistributed. Private, personal-install use of packs legitimately downloaded is not redistribution and
needs no grant. The authors below already said yes to AlifeSpooks; extending each grant to AlifeAmbience
is a one-line request (see the message at the end), and Public-Domain / Creative-Commons / GSC sources
need no extension at all.

## Principle

GSC owns the original S.T.A.L.K.E.R. game audio, and the scene uses it under GSC's long tolerance of
modding. A pack that re-encodes or remasters GSC material does not own it. Any sound that exists in
vanilla Anomaly or in the standalone builds is GSC-original and free of a pack author's claim. A pack
author's terms govern only the sound they created from scratch. The moddb License field is a coarse
default; the record below reads each author's own words.
