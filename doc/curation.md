# Curation

Every sound is assigned by hand. I inspect each source folder and file,
verify content by name and path (by measurement where the name is ambiguous),
and assign its channel, role, and tier. Regex only fetches a group once I have
verified its files are homogeneous; mixed or misfiled sources are assigned
individually. Dead, off-rate, and duplicate files are rejected by measurement.
Dread tier comes from the source, never from an acoustic score.
